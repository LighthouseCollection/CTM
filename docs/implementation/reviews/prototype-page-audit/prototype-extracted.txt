357: The Clerical Tribunal of the Coptic Orthodox Diocese of Los Angeles, having been duly convened under the authority of His Eminence Metropolitan Serapion, and having received and examined the petition of Jessica Marie O'Brien against Patrick James O'Brien regarding the dissolution of their ecclesiastical marriage, hereby issues the following decree.
363: Findings of Fact
364: Approved
366: The Tribunal finds the following facts established through testimony and evidence presented:
366: 1. The parties were joined in holy matrimony on June 12, 2010 at St. Mark Coptic Orthodox Church, Los Angeles, officiated by Fr. Antony Attallah.
366: 2. The respondent, Patrick James O'Brien, engaged in an extramarital relationship beginning approximately March 2024, as admitted in his testimony of April 8, 2025.
366: 3. The applicant discovered the infidelity in September 2024 and sought pastoral counsel from Fr. Youhana Ibrahim.
366: 4. Reconciliation was attempted through three sessions of marriage counseling between August and October 2024. The respondent declined to continue after the third session.
366: 5. Fr. Youhana Ibrahim submitted a written statement on October 28, 2024 confirming that all pastoral avenues for reconciliation had been exhausted.
366: 6. The civil divorce was finalized on January 6, 2025 at the Los Angeles Superior Court.
372: Canonical Basis
373: Approved
375: The petition is brought on the ground of infidelity, which is recognized as a valid ground for the dissolution of an ecclesiastical marriage under the canonical tradition of the Coptic Orthodox Church, in accordance with the Lord's teaching in the Gospel of St. Matthew (19:9).
381: Decree Statement
382: Approved
384: Having carefully examined the testimony of both parties, the documentary evidence presented, the written statement of Fr. Youhana Ibrahim confirming the exhaustion of all reconciliation efforts, and the requisition report prepared by Fr. Gawargious Kotta, the Tribunal hereby grants the petition for the dissolution of the ecclesiastical marriage between Jessica Marie O'Brien and Patrick James O'Brien.
384: The respondent's admission of the extramarital relationship during testimony, together with the corroborating evidence, satisfies the evidentiary standard required under canonical procedure. The Tribunal is satisfied that the ground of infidelity has been established and that all due pastoral and procedural steps were followed prior to this decree.
390: Conditions & Directives
391: Approved
393: 1. The applicant, Jessica Marie O'Brien, is permitted to remarry within the Coptic Orthodox Church following a period of spiritual preparation as directed by her Father of Confession.
393: 2. The respondent, Patrick James O'Brien, shall undergo a period of repentance and spiritual counseling with his Father of Confession, Fr. Antony Attallah, before any future marriage within the Church may be considered. The duration and nature of this period shall be determined by Fr. Antony Attallah in consultation with His Eminence.
393: 3. Both parties are encouraged to continue to prioritize the spiritual welfare and upbringing of their children within the Orthodox faith.
