Case timeline: events grouped by year

The concept from the reference: each area of a case has its own colour (marriage, application, filing, assignment, interviews and hearings, council decision, decree, communications), and events are grouped by year. The colours are new, chosen to sit with the navy, gold and porcelain already decided (D-0050), and each works in light and dark. Use the options for how strongly the colour shows, and click the legend to hide areas. Fictional demo case.
