Key Dates: six designs

The same five milestones of a fictional case, each in the decided card frame. Level 1 is the focal point (the date), level 2 the event, level 3 the status. Done items sit on porcelain, the current item on soft navy, upcoming items on a dashed outline. Use the options to change order and how status shows, then tell me the letter you like.

Colours follow D-0050 (porcelain, navy, gold family). Fictional demo data.
