# Arabic proposals for owner review

New Arabic UI words, added by `ctm3-i18n-engineer` when they ship (decision
D-0012). The owner reviews in batches; a correction becomes a decision and a
glossary change. Never delete rows: mark them `approved` or `corrected`.

Decision D-0037 (2026-11-06): a word that is the term of the first standard that
covers it (1 Microsoft, 2 IBM, 3 Adobe, SAP, GitHub, Atlassian or ISO) is
`approved — Microsoft (D-0037)`, or `corrected — Microsoft: <term> (D-0037)` when
the proposal differed. Church, legal, tribunal and institutional terms stay
`proposed — needs owner/institution` (#120). Rows with several keys list a status
per key.
