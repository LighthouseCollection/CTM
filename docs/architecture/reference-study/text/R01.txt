DIOCESE OF  EL  PASO

The purpose of this questionnaire is to assist you in the initial step of presenting your case to the Tribunal. 
You are petitioning the Tribunal of the Diocese of El Paso to undertake an investigation of your marriage and to 
make a judgment as to whether or not the Catholic community considers you free to enter another marriage. We ask 
you to please complete this questionnaire as carefully and as completely as you possibly can. All the documents and 
information requested from you are important to the Tribunal, since it will be used to determine if your case can be 
accepted and what type of case you have. 
 
Keep in mind  that your submission of this form does not guarantee an automatic declaration of 
nullity of your marriage. If, however, a declaration of nullity is eventually granted, it will have no bearing on 
such things as the legitimacy of children, custody of children, child support, alimony, or visitation rights. These 
are natural and civil  elements of marriage, and this process deals exclusively with the religious and 
sacramental elements. 
 
The Tribunal process is governed by a detailed and strict procedural law of the Catholic Church. Most cases 
are completed in approximately 1 to 2 years, we don’t guarantee that a particular case will be completed at a certain 
time nor that an affirmative decision will be handed down. If problems arise in getting testimonies or if we lose 
contact with you, your case may possibly be delayed. DO NOT SET A DATE FOR FUTURE MARRIAGE IN 
THE CATHOLIC CHURCH while the Tribunal procedure is pending. These  uncertainties often bring 
frustration and doubt that requires great patience. Please understand, however, that our painstaking attention to 
technicalities and the details required in the process are a sign of our great respect for marriage. 
 
It is of utmost importance that you provide the Tribunal with the current and complete address of your 
former spouse, the Respondent. Since marriage involves two people and the Tribunal’s decision will affect both 
parties, we cannot begin a case without information concerning both spouses. The Church’s law requires that your 
former spouse be notified of these proceedings and be invited to participate as an equal participant. If we are 
unable to learn the current address of your former spouse, we may not be able to accept your case. If you do not 
provide the Tribunal with the correct and current address of the Respondent, this may possibly delay your case. 
Hence, it  is YOUR RESPONSIBILITY AS PETITIONER TO PROVIDE A COMPLETE NAME, 
CURRENT ADDRESS AND TELEPHONE NUMBER of your former spouse.
