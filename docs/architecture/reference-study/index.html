Scope boundaries

Priest-report ideas are a new, narrow request for review; they do not create a general witness interview or login feature. No baptism requirement, Catholic canonical procedure, disclosure of one applicant’s material to the other, new fee or new grounds are imported. Sensitive topics are optional institutional-review proposals, not automatic form requirements.
