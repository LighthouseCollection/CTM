# Research prompts for interview question refinement

Use these with another AI if deeper research is needed. Send the master prompt and the relevant category/question prompt, plus the anonymized question bank. No real applicant identities, case evidence, recordings or medical records are needed. These prompts are not an instruction to publish new questions automatically.

## Master prompt

```text
You are helping refine CTM, an Orthodox church tribunal application, not providing a diagnosis, treatment plan, legal advice or a canonical judgment.

Use the attached CTM question bank and accepted decisions as the source of existing coverage. We need precise, neutral factual-discovery prompts, not a counseling session or a full personal biography. Research counseling/marital relationship literature only where it adds a defensible factual distinction missing from this bank. Prefer primary peer-reviewed research or official professional/institutional sources; give direct links and distinguish research support from institutional/doctrinal applicability. Do not invent citations or present Catholic procedures as Orthodox rules.

Workflow: petition nature -> template -> reusable common question family -> detailed questions -> attributed answers/evidence -> assembled report. The same family is reused, not copied. Mandatory interview/recording identity opening and shared chronology/source/evidence prompts are already present. Children, Money and Intimacy are explicit families. Do not repeat these core questions inside every category. Medical/psychiatric testing and disclosure needs signed, scoped release; no blanket records request or diagnostic inference. Do not upload real case data or medical reports to an AI.

Applicant submission, manual interview answer, original recording, reviewed transcript, submitted priest report and council finding are separate records. An affair statement is an allegation; a submitted date can be confirmed, not silently overwritten. Never disclose one applicant's private material to the other. No AI extraction into questionnaire answers is approved. No generic communication, multi-ceremony, full prior-marriage interview or separate appeal question module is to be reintroduced; those proposals were rejected. General witness/priest interviews are out of scope; a separately requested free-form priest testimony report is different.

First test whether the existing question or another family already covers the fact. Return NO ADDITION if it does. At most three narrowly justified follow-ups for an identified gap. For each proposed follow-up give: exact wording; family and stable question reference; the concrete fact sought; trigger/skip condition; why it is not redundant; existing question to refine rather than duplicate; source/evidence limits; possible evidence links; and whether wording needs institutional review. No new required fields, grounds, third-party access, proof threshold, diagnosis or legal rule. Explain how a counseling-source idea differs from a neutral tribunal inquiry.

Return English review wording first. Optional Arabic translation must be labeled a draft. Never imply an unknown or unanswered fact is affirmative. Preserve exact versus approximate dates, occurrence versus discovery, direct versus reported knowledge, and statement versus finding. If a research question cannot be supported, say so. Recommendations remain proposals for human review.
```

## Category prompts

### Decision to marry

```text
Family: Decision to marry (DEC). Status: expanded draft for review.
Focus: Separate the actual decision, reasons, communicated agreements and later knowledge; do not decide validity.
Existing questions:
DEC-01: How did the decision to marry arise, and what were your reasons at that time?
DEC-02: What did you understand your responsibilities would be, and what had you actually discussed together?
DEC-03: Was a specific promise, agreement or condition important to your decision? Identify what was said, by whom and when.
DEC-04: Did what you understood differ from what you later learned? Identify the fact, the discovery and supporting information.
Apply the master prompt. Compare all shared core/families before suggesting changes. Return no addition if the existing coverage is sufficient.
```
