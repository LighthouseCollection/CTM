# CTM — Context-Sensitive Investigative Questions

**Companion to:** CTM Question Bank (59 interview questions)  
**Status:** Draft for review — no published question definitions changed  
**Purpose:** Factual discovery for church divorce/annulment inquiries, **not** marriage counseling, therapy, or automated canonical determination.

## How to use this companion

The **existing question wording below is reproduced unchanged** from the supplied CTM bank. Prompts are optional aids to the assigned interviewer, **not** additional mandatory questions, fields, or evidence thresholds. Before asking a prompt, check the application, prior answer, event chronology, and any appropriately accessible submitted evidence. Ask only for a material fact that remains unanswered. Record each event and source item once; cross-reference it elsewhere. Preserve separate spouses’ accounts and exact source versions.

A prompt must never be automatically addressed to the other applicant, nor disclose another applicant’s private material. For medical/psychiatric records, a report or request is not a signed release: verify the applicable signed, versioned, purpose/recipient-scoped authorization before disclosure, search, or report inclusion. Do not send medical documents to external AI. Where abuse or protective orders are implicated, prioritize safe, separate interviewing and authorized disclosure.

**Council review is separate:** prompts establish attributed assertions and their sources; the Council subsequently assesses material assertions, contrary information, evidentiary limitations, and ecclesiastical significance. Do not assign a credibility score to an applicant or presume a canonical ground has been established.

### Coverage key

- **No routine prompts:** The original question is sufficient; use a follow-up only if an exceptional, case-specific ambiguity arises.
- **Conditional prompts:** Use none, one, or several depending on the factual gap. The trigger is a guide, not an automatic decision rule.
- **Avoid/reuse:** Prevent duplicate questioning by referring to earlier TIM, CON, MED, family answers and existing evidence references.

## Question-by-question companion

## Foundational

### OPEN-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Please state your full name and your spouse’s full name.

**Trigger:** Only if identity conflicts with an existing record.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Which name appears on the relevant marriage or identity record?

**Avoid / reuse:** Administrative identity verification, not substantive questioning.

### BASIS-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What are you asking the council to decide? Briefly identify the main facts supporting your request; the details will be addressed in the relevant sections.

**Trigger:** If the request or core factual allegation is unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What specific event or circumstance is central to the decision you are requesting?
- Which part of that account do you personally know?

**Avoid / reuse:** Route detailed facts to the appropriate substantive family.

## Courtship / engagement

### BKG-02

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> How did your relationship develop from first meeting through engagement? Give a brief outline of the main events; describe issues covered in later sections there rather than repeating them here.

**Trigger:** No routine follow-up.

**Additional prompts:** None recommended as routine.

**Avoid / reuse:** Keep as a brief timeline outline; use DEC, PRS, DBT or CON for details.

### BKG-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Were there any other circumstances during courtship or engagement relevant to your request that are not covered in the later sections? If so, describe them.

**Trigger:** Only if a new relevant circumstance is identified but not described elsewhere.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What specific circumstance has not yet been covered, and when did it occur?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not repeat already documented courtship events.

## Decision to marry

### DEC-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> How did the decision to marry arise, and what were your reasons at that time?

**Trigger:** If the origin of the decision or the interviewee’s contemporaneous reasons remain unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Who first proposed marriage, and what was your response at that time?
- Was there a particular event that changed your decision?

**Avoid / reuse:** Pressure belongs in PRS; doubts in DBT; promises in DEC-03.

### DEC-02

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Before marriage, what did you understand your responsibilities in married life would be? Describe any relevant expectations or agreements not covered in the questions about children, intimate life or finances.

**Trigger:** Only where a particular expectation is material and not yet specified.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What did you understand was expected of you, and how did you come to that understanding?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use CHD, INT-02 and MON-01 for topic-specific expectations.

### DEC-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Apart from matters already discussed or addressed in the questions about children, intimate life or finances, was any other specific promise, agreement or condition important to your decision to marry? What was said, by whom and when, and how did the other person respond? Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If an identified promise or condition lacks specificity.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What exactly was promised or required?
- Was that expressed before the decision to marry, and what response followed?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not reproduce CON questions unless misrepresentation or concealment is alleged.

### DEC-04

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Apart from matters already discussed, did you later learn anything that differed from your understanding when you decided to marry? Briefly identify the difference and when you learned it. Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If an asserted difference between prior understanding and later knowledge is vague.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What was your understanding at the time, and what later fact differed?

**Avoid / reuse:** Collect detailed alleged concealment under CON; use topic-specific section otherwise.

## Pressure and threats

### PRS-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Did any pressure, threat or other circumstance affect your decision to marry?

**Trigger:** No separate prompt needed: screening question.

**Additional prompts:** None recommended as routine.

**Avoid / reuse:** If yes, use PRS-02–04.

### PRS-02

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> If so, identify the source and describe what happened and when. Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If described pressure is conclusory rather than an identifiable act.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What exactly was said or done, and by whom?
- What consequence, if any, was communicated if you refused or postponed the marriage?

**Avoid / reuse:** Dates already in the original question; do not ask again if supplied.

### PRS-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> How did that pressure, threat or circumstance affect your decision, and what did you communicate about it at the time?

**Trigger:** If the link between the conduct and the decision is unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What did you understand your available choices to be at that time?
- Did you communicate a wish to refuse or postpone? What was said and how did the other person respond?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not assume the person was unable to consent.

### PRS-04

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What information or evidence from that time supports your account? Identify anything material that remains uncertain.

**Trigger:** If contemporaneous information is alluded to but not identified.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Was your concern communicated or recorded at the time? If so, where is that account documented?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use existing evidence links; no compulsory corroboration threshold.

**Trigger:** No routine follow-up.

### DBT-02

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Did you discuss those doubts or concerns? Identify what you said, to whom and what response or action followed, to the extent not already explained.

**Trigger:** Only if an important contemporaneous discussion is unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

**Avoid / reuse:** Avoid repeating PRS-03 where the same exchange is already documented.

### DBT-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

**Trigger:** If the stated reason for proceeding is too general to establish the decision at the time.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What changed, if anything, between expressing the concern and proceeding with the wedding?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not imply that proceeding proves free consent.

## Misrepresentation or concealment

### CON-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Before marriage, do you believe someone gave you false information or withheld an important fact, including financial information? If so, identify the fact and the person involved.

**Trigger:** No routine follow-up: screening and allegation identification.

**Additional prompts:** None recommended as routine.

**Avoid / reuse:** If yes, use CON-02–05; do not presume a falsehood occurred.

### CON-02

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What specifically were you told, by whom and when? If the fact was not disclosed, what, if anything, had been asked or discussed about that subject? Include any relevant financial statement or agreement. Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If the alleged statement or omission has not been particularized.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What words do you recall, as closely as possible?
- What specifically was asked or discussed about the undisclosed subject before marriage?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not demand an exact quote if memory is approximate.

### CON-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What did you believe about that matter when deciding to marry, and why was it important to your decision?

**Trigger:** If the claim of importance to consent is only a conclusion.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What decision or expectation did you form because of that understanding?
- What do you believe you would have decided if you had known the alleged fact at the time?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not let a retrospective answer substitute for contemporaneous knowledge.

### CON-04

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What did you later learn that differed from your earlier understanding, and when and how did you learn it? Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If the established fact, source, or date of discovery is unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What information first led you to believe your earlier understanding was inaccurate?
- Does that information show the circumstance existed before marriage or only later?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use TIM for occurrence versus discovery if already recorded.

### CON-05

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What evidence supports this account, and what remains uncertain or disputed? Identify each supporting item and explain which fact it supports, without re-identifying items already recorded.

**Trigger:** If it is unclear which specific assertion an item supports.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Which particular statement or date does this item support?
- Is there contextual information that could affect its interpretation?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use exact existing EVD references; do not require new uploads.

## Infidelity

### INF-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Describe the affair or adultery you are reporting, including the specific actions or communications. Who was involved, and when and where did it happen, if known? Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If conduct is alleged using only a label, or a reported admission/denial remains materially unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What specific actions or communications are you alleging?
- Has your spouse acknowledged, denied, or explained the particular conduct? If so, what was said, when, and in what circumstances?
- Does any part of the alleged conduct predate the church marriage? On what basis?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use INF-02 for discovery, INF-03 for source of knowledge, INF-04 for evidence. An admission report is not automatically a proven fact.

### INF-02

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> When and how did you first learn about the reported affair or adultery? Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If discovery and alleged occurrence have been conflated.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What was the first information you encountered, and how did you encounter it?
- Does the date you gave refer to discovery or to the alleged conduct itself?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use TIM if chronology is already resolved.

### INF-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Which parts of your account did you personally experience or observe, which came from another source, and which are your interpretation or suspicion? Clarify only what is not already clear.

**Trigger:** Only if the basis of a particular material allegation remains ambiguous.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- For that particular fact, what did you observe yourself, what did another person report, and what is your own inference?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not ask the applicant to retell the whole INF-01 narrative.

### INF-04

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> What specific evidence supports your account? Identify each supporting item and explain which fact it supports, using existing references for items already recorded.

**Trigger:** If the submitted item or the proposition it supports is unclear.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Which exact message, document, or recording are you referring to?
- What does it show directly, and what are you concluding from it?

**Avoid / reuse:** Use existing EVD source/version reference; avoid demanding duplicate files or assuming authenticity.

## Abuse

### ABU-01

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Describe the abuse you are reporting. What happened, who was involved, and when and where did it happen? Was it a single incident or repeated behavior? Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

**Trigger:** If a general label conceals distinct events or a possible pattern.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Describe one specific incident in sequence: what happened first, next, and afterward?

### ABU-02

**Trigger:** Only if an important assertion has no clear basis of knowledge.

**Avoid / reuse:** Skip where ABU-01 already separates sources.

### ABU-03

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

**Trigger:** If an asserted effect remains unspecified.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- What particular change or event followed the reported conduct that is relevant to the petition?

**Avoid / reuse:** Do not require emotional or therapeutic narrative beyond relevant factual effects.

### ABU-04

**Existing wording (unchanged):**

> Are there existing court orders, reports or other evidence you wish to identify? Explain which fact each item supports, using existing references for items already recorded.

**Trigger:** If an order or report is mentioned without its relevant meaning being identified.

**Optional factual clarification:**

- Does any current order affect safe contact or disclosure?

**Avoid / reuse:** Respect protective orders and restricted records; existing evidence references only.

## Council assessment guide (not applicant questions)

For each **material assertion**, consider: What exact event is alleged? What is the interviewee’s knowledge basis? Which dates refer to occurrence versus discovery? Which exact authorized item supports which proposition? What context or conflicting material limits the inference? What remains genuinely unknown? Is a further clarification proportionate and useful? Record a reasoned institutional assessment separately from the interview response and separately from the priest’s free-form testimony.

Do not force mutually exclusive labels such as “supported,” “disputed,” and “needs follow-up”; these can coexist. Do not convert a document upload into proof, an observation into a diagnosis, or an allegation into a canonical finding.

## Coverage and change control

- All **59 active IDs** in the supplied bank are included once and in source order; retired IDs are not reactivated.
- Existing question wording is retained verbatim in each entry; these are companion prompts only.
- No mandatory additions, new canonical grounds, general witness-interview module, child-testimony module, or evidence-upload rules are authorized by this document.
- Proposals should be reconciled against established IDs, bilingual versions, and Council policy before adoption.
