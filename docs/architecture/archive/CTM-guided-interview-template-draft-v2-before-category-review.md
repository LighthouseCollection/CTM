# Guided applicant interview: structured factual discovery

Template: `INT-APPLICANT`, draft revision 2. Proposed wording for institutional review, not a published template or a statement of canonical eligibility. Use separately for either applicant. This revision replaces the earlier flat draft with categorized questions; any future published versions remain immutable. Display numbers indicate order; stable question codes identify meaning.

## Interview approach

Establish who is speaking, what outcome they seek, and on what stated basis before investigating events. Confirm existing application information rather than asking the applicant to repeat the entire form. Investigate each stated ground separately using specific events, dates, direct knowledge and supporting evidence. Distinguish allegations, recollection, documents and council findings. Do not imply that selecting divorce or annulment establishes entitlement or decides the case type.

Every interview has its own identity opening. Every separately started recording includes that opening as well. Routine pause/resume within the same recording does not require repeating the entire interview. Ordinary interview recording remains optional; when recording is used, preserve the informed-about-recording record before capture and repeat its acknowledgement in the opening. No new oath, applicant signature or mandatory admission is introduced.

## 1. Opening and identity — every interview and recording

Staff associates the event with the case, applicant, interviewer, date/time and method. Where recorded, the interviewer states the case reference, date and interviewer identity in the audio opening. Avoid unnecessary sensitive identity details in the recording.

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN-01 | Please state your full name. | يُرجى ذكر اسمك الكامل. |
| OPEN-02 | Please state the full name of your spouse in this case. | يُرجى ذكر الاسم الكامل للطرف الآخر في هذه القضية. |
| OPEN-03 | If recording: you have been informed that this interview is being recorded. Please confirm that you have been informed. | عند التسجيل: لقد أُبلغت بأن هذه المقابلة تُسجَّل. يُرجى تأكيد علمك بذلك. |

Identity statements are tied to the registered case participant; they do not by themselves change identity records. If the stated person does not match the intended participant, resolve the mismatch before attributing substantive answers to them.

## 2. What the applicant seeks and on what basis

Ask this before the account of events. It determines which facts need exploration, not the council's ultimate decision.

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| SEEK-01 | What are you asking the council to grant in this case: divorce/dissolution, annulment/declaration of nullity, or another outcome? | ما الذي تطلب من المجلس إقراره في هذه القضية: الطلاق أو حل الزواج، أم بطلان الزواج، أم نتيجة أخرى؟ |
| SEEK-02 | What grounds or reasons do you rely on for that request? Please identify each separately. | ما الأسباب التي تستند إليها في هذا الطلب؟ يُرجى ذكر كل سبب على حدة. |
| SEEK-03 | Does that request or its basis differ from what you submitted in your application? If so, explain the difference. | هل يختلف هذا الطلب أو أساسه عما ورد في طلبك المقدَّم؟ إذا كان كذلك، يُرجى توضيح الفرق. |

Record requested outcome and claimed grounds as applicant statements, separate from council-established type/grounds, votes and approval. For an appeal, also identify the decision challenged and the new information/evidence relied on; do not overwrite the original request.

## 3. Confirm information submitted in the application

Use the applicant's own submitted version as the reference. Identity confirmation above is separate from correction/clarification of application fields here.

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| APP-01 | Are any personal or contact details in your application incorrect or in need of clarification? Identify the specific detail and explain. | هل توجد بيانات شخصية أو بيانات اتصال في طلبك غير صحيحة أو تحتاج إلى إيضاح؟ يُرجى تحديد البيان وتوضيحه. |
| APP-02 | Please confirm the marriage date, church or venue, and the marriage documents submitted. Identify any discrepancy. | يُرجى تأكيد تاريخ الزواج والكنيسة أو مكان إتمامه ووثائق الزواج المقدَّمة، وبيان أي اختلاف. |
| APP-03 | Have any relevant facts changed since you submitted the application or last spoke with the council member? | هل تغيَّرت أي وقائع ذات صلة منذ تقديم الطلب أو آخر حديث مع عضو المجلس؟ |

Reference exact fields and evidence versions. Interview answers do not automatically update signed application answers, account profile details or established case links. Corrections follow existing authorized/history-preserving actions. No side-by-side display requirement is introduced.

## 4. Marriage background and sequence of events

Establish the sequence before drawing conclusions. Explore the periods relevant to the stated request; do not assume every marriage requires every follow-up.

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| TIME-01 | When did the difficulties relevant to your request first arise? Describe what happened then. | متى بدأت الصعوبات المرتبطة بطلبك؟ يُرجى وصف ما حدث في ذلك الوقت. |
| TIME-02 | What happened next? Identify the significant events in date order, including separation where relevant. | ماذا حدث بعد ذلك؟ يُرجى ذكر الأحداث المهمة بترتيبها الزمني، بما فيها الانفصال إن كان ذا صلة. |
| TIME-03 | Which dates are exact, which are approximate, and which do you not know? | ما التواريخ المؤكدة، وما التواريخ التقريبية، وما التواريخ التي لا تعرفها؟ |

Where the request concerns facts before marriage, establish what existed then, what the applicant knew at the time and when later information became known. Timing alone is not a finding of canonical grounds.

## 5. Examine each stated ground or reason

Repeat this block for each claimed ground. Label each block with its ground and its own question/answer/evidence references. Do not combine different allegations into a single established conclusion.

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| FACT-01 | For this ground, describe the specific event or conduct you rely on. When and where did it happen? | بالنسبة إلى هذا السبب، صِف الواقعة أو التصرف المحدد الذي تستند إليه. متى وأين حدث؟ |
| FACT-02 | What did you personally see, hear or experience, and what did you learn from another source? | ما الذي شاهدته أو سمعته أو عشته بنفسك، وما الذي عرفته من مصدر آخر؟ |
| FACT-03 | If you learned it later, when did you learn it, and how? | إذا علمت بذلك لاحقًا، فمتى وكيف علمت به؟ |
| FACT-04 | Was this a single event or repeated conduct? Identify the relevant occasions or period. | هل كانت هذه واقعة واحدة أم تصرفًا متكررًا؟ يُرجى تحديد الوقائع أو الفترة ذات الصلة. |
| FACT-05 | Is there any uncertainty, missing context or information that may differ from your account? | هل يوجد أي أمر غير مؤكد أو سياق ناقص أو معلومات قد تختلف عن روايتك؟ |

Case-specific follow-up examples, used only where relevant:

- For alleged pre-marriage misrepresentation/concealment: what was represented or not disclosed, by whom, when, what was known before marriage, what was learned later, and what supports that account?
- For alleged abandonment: when separation began, known circumstances, relevant contact and reconciliation efforts, distinguishing known facts from assumptions about the spouse's intentions.
- For other claimed grounds: use the same event/date/source/evidence structure. Do not add diagnoses, automatic conclusions or new canonical eligibility tests.

These are follow-up prompts within applicant interviews, not a priest/witness interview feature or a request to create accounts for third parties.

## 6. Supporting evidence and factual discrepancies

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| EVID-01 | Which submitted document or other evidence supports each event you described? Explain the connection. | ما الوثيقة أو الدليل المقدَّم الذي يدعم كل واقعة ذكرتها؟ يُرجى توضيح الصلة. |
| EVID-02 | Who created the item, when was it created, and how did you obtain it, if you know? | من أنشأ هذا المستند أو الدليل، ومتى أُنشئ، وكيف حصلت عليه، إن كنت تعرف؟ |
| EVID-03 | For this specific inconsistency identified by the interviewer, what is your explanation? | ما تفسيرك لهذا الاختلاف المحدد الذي أشار إليه المحاور؟ |
| EVID-04 | Is there additional information or evidence you intend to provide? Identify what it relates to. | هل توجد معلومات أو أدلة إضافية تنوي تقديمها؟ يُرجى تحديد ما تتعلق به. |

Link exact retained evidence versions to the relevant question/event. Preserve differing assertions rather than overwriting either account. The interviewer must respect applicant isolation: no automatic display, sharing or quoting of the other applicant's private submission. Discuss discrepancies only within authorized disclosure boundaries; this prompt is not a new cross-applicant disclosure permission.

## 7. Reconciliation efforts and current relevant position

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| CONTEXT-01 | What attempts at reconciliation were made, when, and what happened? | ما محاولات المصالحة التي جرت، ومتى، وما نتائجها؟ |
| CONTEXT-02 | Are there reported civil proceedings, divorce documents or court orders that require factual clarification? | هل توجد إجراءات مدنية أو وثائق طلاق أو أوامر قضائية سبق ذكرها وتحتاج إلى إيضاح الوقائع المتعلقة بها؟ |
| CONTEXT-03 | Is there other current information directly relevant to the request that has not yet been covered? | هل توجد معلومات حالية أخرى ذات صلة مباشرة بالطلب ولم تُناقَش بعد؟ |

No new directory, testimony from children or assessment of future marriage eligibility is introduced. Reconciliation here is factual background; withdrawal remains a no-reason/no-approval action under existing rules.

## 8. Closing clarification and outstanding information

| Code | English prompt | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| CLOSE-01 | Is there anything you said today that you would like to clarify or correct? | هل يوجد أي شيء ذكرته اليوم وتود إيضاحه أو تصحيحه؟ |
| CLOSE-02 | Is there any additional fact relevant to your request that we have not discussed? | هل توجد واقعة إضافية ذات صلة بطلبك لم نناقشها؟ |

Staff records outstanding information, evidence links and any applicant-facing request through the existing case correspondence process. Information received later is flagged New; no Request completed gate. Closing the interview is not closing the case, issuing a ruling or requiring a new applicant signature.

## Separate internal discovery assessment

Keep this outside the applicant's answers and the verbatim transcript:

- Requested outcome and each alleged ground, attributed to applicant.
- Timeline of material events with exact/approximate/unknown dates.
- Evidence supporting or contradicting each point, with source/version references.
- Matters established by council versus applicant assertions, uncertain matters and factual gaps.
- Additional questions/information to gather and assigned member observations/recommendations.

Manual answers, audio transcript and council findings are separate records. No AI mapping from transcript into template answers is approved. Optional audio question markers reference stable question code and time. Assigned member reviews generated interview text before wider council text access. Original audio/AI output and every revision remain preserved; closed original proceedings remain read-only.

## Design implications

Category IDs, stable question codes, display order, repeatable ground/event blocks and bilingual text belong to the immutable template version. Interview/recording identity openings have explicit links to their event/capture, not an assumption that an old name answer covers every later recording. Structured entries support date certainty, source-of-knowledge type and exact evidence-version links without forcing a narrative into a finding. No new published requiredness rules, side-by-side UI, canonical outcomes or third-party interview permissions follow from this draft.
