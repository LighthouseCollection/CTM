# Requested priest testimony — free-form report template

User-confirmed purpose: council may request a priest's knowledge of the marriage circumstances through a private link. Priest writes or records free-form testimony. This document defines the template content and boundaries, not an implemented external portal. Institution-authored instructions and the exact link-verification/signature workflow remain for review.

## Explicit report identification

The request pre-populates these fields in the template header; they are not an invitation to browse the case:

| Header | English | العربية |
|---|---|---|
| Case | Case number | رقم القضية |
| Marriage concerned | Marriage of [first name last name] and [first name last name] | الزواج المعني: [الاسم الأول واسم العائلة] و[الاسم الأول واسم العائلة] |
| Report concerns | [named applicant / named other party / both parties, as requested] | التقرير بشأن: [اسم الطرف المعني أو الطرفين بحسب الطلب] |
| Requested by | Actual authorized member/admin/secretary and request date | مُقدِّم الطلب وتاريخه |
| Testimony author | Priest's full name and parish; separately attributed from report subject | اسم الكاهن الكامل وكنيسته، مع تمييزه عن الطرف موضوع التقرير |

The report must explicitly identify whom it concerns. The priest's author identity, the requesting council member and the subject applicant are three different relationships. No birth-date-based applicant reference or unnecessary case information needs to appear in an external link.

## Free-form writing or recording

**Suggested instruction for review:**

Please provide testimony based on your knowledge of the marriage identified above. Explain how you know the parties and which relevant facts you personally observed. Distinguish direct knowledge from information reported to you, and state any limits on your knowledge. Where appropriate, describe relevant non-confessional reconciliation assistance and its observed outcome. If you offer an opinion or recommendation, identify it as such and state its factual basis. Do not include sacramental-confession content.

**صياغة عربية مقترحة للمراجعة:**

يُرجى تقديم شهادتك استنادًا إلى معرفتك بظروف الزواج المحدَّد أعلاه. وضِّح طبيعة معرفتك بالطرفين والوقائع ذات الصلة التي شاهدتها بنفسك. ميِّز بين معرفتك المباشرة والمعلومات التي نُقلت إليك، واذكر حدود معرفتك. وعند الاقتضاء، صِف المساعدة التي قدَّمتها للمصالحة خارج سر الاعتراف والنتيجة التي لاحظتها. وإذا أبديت رأيًا أو توصية، فبيِّن أنها رأيك واذكر الوقائع التي تستند إليها. لا تُدرِج ما يتعلق بسر الاعتراف.

This is one free-form response area, not mandatory individual answers to the applicant interview question bank. Writer may type or record testimony using the request's allowed method. Whether writing and recording may be combined in one submission is not assumed by the either/or requirement.

## Recording opening and processing

At the beginning of each new recording, priest states full name/parish and identifies the marriage and the party or parties the report concerns. Retain original audio unchanged. Apply the agreed source-language transcription, English/Arabic translation and mixed-language handling with timestamps, speaker labels, source/provenance and revision history. Preserve written testimony separately from transcript; no AI-generated answers or replacement of the priest's submitted words.

## Submission record and access

Retain requested subject, attributed author, exact submitted text/audio, request ID, actual submission time and authentication evidence. Approved author/date/signature-or-authenticated-submission intent does not establish an unapproved notary/oath process. The link is scoped to this report only, with minimum subject information; no applicant questionnaires, council notes, case browsing or other-party evidence access. Report material is council-only. AI-generated text requires the appropriate review before general council text access; reviewer assignment and link security lifecycle remain focused decisions.

Any later correction must not overwrite original testimony, source audio or authorship. The exact post-submission correction/resubmission route is not yet decided. A request is a separate report event, not a priest account, a generic witness program or an interview conducted by the council case owner.
