# CTM Question Bank

Current question wording, grouped by category.

Total: 59 interview questions. Administrative fields and interviewer instructions are not included in this total.

## Interviewer instructions

Ask each question only to the extent it has not already been answered. Where an earlier response addresses all or part of a later question, reference that response and ask only for missing information or clarification.

Record each event once. If the event is relevant to another section, refer to the earlier account and obtain only the additional details needed for that section.

Throughout the interview, distinguish:

- What the interviewee personally experienced or observed.
- What someone else told the interviewee.
- What the interviewee inferred or suspects.
- What remains unknown or disputed.

Identify each supporting document or other item once. When it is relevant to another question, use its existing reference and explain any additional fact it supports.

Use the concealment section for allegations of false or withheld information, including financial information. Use the finances section for premarital financial discussions and specific financial decisions or disagreements. Do not obtain the same account in both sections.

## Administrative record

Completed by the interviewer:

- Interviewer’s full name.
- Interview date.
- Case number.
- Where audio recording is used, record that the interviewee was informed about recording.

## Decision to marry

### DEC-01

How did the decision to marry arise, and what were your reasons at that time?

### DEC-02

Before marriage, what did you understand your responsibilities in married life would be? Describe any relevant expectations or agreements not covered in the questions about children, intimate life or finances.

### DEC-03

Apart from matters already discussed or addressed in the questions about children, intimate life or finances, was any other specific promise, agreement or condition important to your decision to marry? What was said, by whom and when, and how did the other person respond? Give exact dates where known; otherwise, say whether the date is approximate or unknown.

### DEC-04

Interviewer note: If the response involves alleged false information or concealment, obtain the remaining details under CON. If another topic-specific section applies, obtain the remaining details there.
